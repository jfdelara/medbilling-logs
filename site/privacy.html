Privacy Policy

Medibrisa MedBilling Logs, by Medibrisa Software Ltd. Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted behind your passcode. We do not collect it, see it or sell it. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

What the app stores

Your log entries (times, places, fee codes, notes, photos and reports) and your settings are stored only on your device, encrypted. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups and sharing

If you make a backup or share a report, the file goes only where you send it (for example to Files, iCloud Drive or an email you write). Backups are encrypted with your passcode.

Subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment card details and do not learn who you are from your purchase.

Patient information

Medibrisa MedBilling Logs is a personal time and billing log, not a medical record. Keep identifying patient details out of your entries unless your own privacy obligations allow it; you are responsible for the information you choose to record.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or clear its data) and everything it stored on the device is gone. We hold no copy.
